# Project Limitations

## Measurement Period

PBJ staffing data covers Q2 2024.

CMS claims-based quality measures cover an annual period ending March 31, 2024.

Therefore, staffing and outcome measures are not from identical measurement periods.

## Causality

The analysis is observational.

Correlations and staffing-group comparisons demonstrate association but do not establish that higher staffing causes better outcomes.

## Missing Quality Scores

CMS adjusted quality scores are unavailable for some facilities. Missing scores are retained as null and are not imputed.

## Provider Matching

17 of 14,564 PBJ facilities did not match the Provider Information dataset.

The pipeline preserves these facilities using a left join.

## Confounding Factors

The analysis does not fully control for factors such as:

- Resident acuity
- Facility case mix
- Geography
- Ownership
- Local labor markets

Future longitudinal analysis across multiple PBJ quarters could provide stronger evidence.
